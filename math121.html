Textbook:

Calculus:Concepts and Contexts by James Stewart. 

You need to buy the "WebAssign + Ebook Access F/ Calculus (New Only)" directly from the Kansas Union Bookstore. See also www.cengagebrain.com/micro/kumath121

This will be your only required "textbook." Be sure that you are getting the "life of edition" version. That means it will still work next semester for MATH 122.

We will cover most parts of Chapters 1 through 6.
